Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. Talk Board is a free communication app for nonspeaking children.

We built Talk Board to help children communicate, and we take privacy seriously — especially because many of our users are children. This policy explains, in plain language, what data the app uses and how.

The short version

  • The talking board works fully on your device. No account is required to use it.
  • Your settings and any voice recordings you make stay on your device by default.
  • We do not show ads and we do not sell data.
  • We do not run third-party analytics or tracking.

Information stored on your device

The following is saved locally in your browser/app storage and never leaves your device unless you choose to contribute it:

  • Settings — language, dialect, and voice preferences (browser localStorage).
  • Personal voice recordings — audio you record for a word, stored in IndexedDB on the device.
  • Pending word suggestions — words you suggest, held locally until reviewed.

Clearing your browser/app data removes all of the above.

Microphone

The app asks for microphone access only when you tap a record button, and only to save the recording you make. Audio is never streamed or recorded in the background.

Community contributions (optional)

If you choose to contribute a word or dialect recording to the shared community library, that word and audio are uploaded to our backend (Supabase) and reviewed by a moderator before anyone else can see them. Contributed audio is intended to be shared publicly within the app to help other families. Only contribute recordings you have the right to share, and do not include personal or identifying information.

Contributors may sign in with an email address so we can attribute and moderate submissions. We use this only for running the contribution and review system.

Children's privacy

The core board requires no account and collects no personal information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Account creation and contribution features are intended for parents, caregivers, and educators.

Data we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, photos, or files beyond the audio you explicitly record.

Service providers

When community features are enabled, we use Supabase to store contributed words and audio. Supabase processes this data on our behalf.

Your choices

  • Use the app without an account — nothing is uploaded.
  • Delete local data by clearing the app's storage.
  • Request removal of a community contribution by contacting us.
