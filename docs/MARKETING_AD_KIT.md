## Audience targeting notes

| Platform | Suggested targeting |
|----------|---------------------|
| **Parents** | Parents of children ages 2–12; interests: parenting, special needs, early childhood |
| **AAC / autism** | Interests: autism awareness, speech therapy, AAC, occupational therapy, special education |
| **Arabic / Sudan** | Languages: Arabic; locations: Sudan, South Sudan, Egypt, Gulf states, diaspora (US/UK/Canada Arabic speakers); interests: Sudanese culture, Juba Arabic |
| **Educators** | Job titles: speech-language pathologist, special education teacher, occupational therapist, school counselor |
| **Exclusions** | Avoid medical-device claims; do not target children directly — target caregivers 18+ |

**Brand voice:** Warm, practical, respectful. Emphasize free, offline, dialect voices, privacy. Never invent testimonials or clinical outcomes.

## Short video scripts

### 15-second hook (EN)

```
[0–3s]  On screen: child-facing board with picture cards.
        VO: "What if your child could speak — by tapping a picture?"

[3–8s]  Tap "water" → word plays. Tap "mom" → recorded voice plays.
        VO: "Talk Board. Free. Works offline."

[8–12s] Show dialect/language setting. Tap ⭐ to pin a word to home.
        VO: "Arabic, Sudanese, Juba — or your own voice."

[12–15s] Logo + CTA card: "Try free — housegames.club/amoory"
        Text overlay: Free · No ads
```

### 30-second script (EN)

```
[0–5s]   Caregiver POV: frustration with generic AAC app wrong language.
         VO: "Most communication apps don't sound like your home."

[5–12s]  Open Talk Board. Large cards. Child taps "eat" → dialect TTS.
         VO: "Talk Board lets nonspeaking children tap pictures to speak —
               in Arabic, Sudanese, or Juba dialect."

[12–20s] Caregiver holds ⚙️, records voice for "love". Child taps → mom's voice.
         VO: "Record your voice for any word. No account needed."

[20–26s] Phone in airplane mode — board still works. Child taps pinned favorites.
         VO: "Works offline. Pin the words they use every day."

[26–30s] End card: Talk Board logo, "Try free" URL, privacy line.
         VO: "Talk Board — free, private, built for families like yours."
```

### 15-second hook (AR)

```
[0–3s]  لوحة صور كبيرة على الشاشة.
        صوت: "طفلك يقدر يتواصل — بضغطة صورة."

[3–10s] ضغط على "ماء" ثم "أم" — صوت مسجّل.
        صوت: "لوحة الحديث. مجاني. يعمل دون اتصال."

[10–15s] شعار + "جرّبه مجاناً"
```
